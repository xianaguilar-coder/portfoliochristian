The Challenge

BanBajío had scale, credibility and legacy, but its voice was outdated and disconnected from digital-native audiences. The issue wasn't just aesthetics, it was relevancy. Messaging didn't reflect the bank's ambition or engage the audiences it needed to grow.

My task was to close that gap: build a strategic content system with a human tone, clear editorial structure, and brand behavior aligned with the bank's true market stature.

My Approach

This wasn't about launching content. It was about fixing the system behind it. I led a full strategic repositioning that included:

  • Audience StrategyDiagnosed the brand–audience disconnect through benchmarking and behavioral analysis, aligned content with segmented personas and platform context.
  • Voice & MessagingReframed the bank's tone to speak to real people, not just profiles, and established a clear messaging hierarchy for consistency across channels.
  • Editorial SystemDefined content pillars, tones, and modular formats to enable consistent, scalable storytelling.
  • Team EnablementCreated guidelines to align cross-functional teams, both internal and client-side, enabling speed, cohesion, and stakeholder clarity.
  • Brand SettingLed a visual identity refresh applied beyond content, influencing the bank's broader communication system and brand behavior.

How I Drove the Solution

Diagnose the Disconnect

Identified the strategic disconnect between BanBajío's market stature and its digital behavior, outdated tone, fragmented formats, and low relevance with key audiences.

Locate the Opportunity

Analyzed competitor tone, user behavior, and content patterns to define where BanBajío could differentiate and lead.

Takeaways

  • Strategic clarity enables growth, even in rigid industries like banking. I identified overlooked opportunities and built systems that scaled.
  • Voice and identity aren't aesthetic choices, they shape perception. Repositioning BanBajío improved recognition and modernized its retail presence.
  • Content doesn't need to go viral to perform. Reels with strategic depth drove retention and community without compromising brand credibility.
  • Cross-functional teams aligned through actionable workflows, with direct client engagement and vision translated from brief to launch.
